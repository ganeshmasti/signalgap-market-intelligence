# Data Provenance and Limitations

- Original snapshot files: user-supplied `validation_summary.json`, `direction_predictions_{5,20}d.csv`, `regime_predictions_{5,20}d.csv`, `spectral_features.csv`.
- The prediction snapshots are based on a small user-selected universe of 5 AI-related U.S. equities with SPY as the benchmark; the source report marks the as-of date **2026-11-20**.
- The separate spectral reference series was generated from 6 assets (including SPY) and should not be interpreted as the five-stock backtesting feature series.
- This is a frozen research artifact; no auto-refresh or market-price feed is connected.
- Observations and outcomes overlap, making naive accuracy uncertainty estimates misleading.
- No model-generated probabilities in this site are live recommendations or evidence of a durable trading advantage.
- Raw downloaded adjusted stock-price histories are not redistributed in this package.
